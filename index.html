Economics, tested on real companies

Econspire began as a student economics community. Our first case competition was in Bukhara on 1 March, on a live case from O'zbegim, with a judge from O'zbegim itself.

Since then we have run case competitions, debates, lectures and webinars in cities across the country, and more than 5,000 students have taken part. Students organise all of it, with local volunteers and regional ambassadors.

We want every city in Uzbekistan to host a case competition at least once a month, so a student in Karshi gets the same chance to be tested as a student in Tashkent.

The Econspire team

How a round works

  1. 1

    Form a team

    Two to five students, signed up for a competition in your city.

  2. 2

    Get the case

    A real economic or business problem, often set by a local company.

  3. 3

    Present

    Pitch your solution to a panel of judges that includes people from industry.

  4. 4

    Get feedback

    Every team gets feedback and a certificate. Top teams win prizes, books and internships.
